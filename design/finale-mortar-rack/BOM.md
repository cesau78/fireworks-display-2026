## Field setup



- Same row angles on both racks: **0°, ±6°, ±12°**.

- **25 tubes per rack** — map to shells 47–71 and 72–96, or mirror layout as you wire the fuse.

- Racks are smaller for **transport**; set side by side on site to restore fan width.



OpenSCAD `rack.scad` models **one** rack; build two from the same cut list.
